# EDA Questions & Hypotheses

## Business Questions
1. Which product category generates the highest total sales?
2. Which region performs best in terms of total sales?
3. How do sales change month by month?
4. Do returning customers have higher average sales than new customers?
5. Is there a relationship between delivery time and customer satisfaction?
6. Does discount level appear to be associated with sales?
7. Which variables contain missing values?
8. Are there duplicate records?
9. Are there unusual values/outliers that may affect further analysis?

## Hypothesis
**H0:** There is no difference in average sales between new and returning customers.

**H1:** Average sales are different between new and returning customers.

Significance level: 0.05

## Assumptions to Check
- Numeric fields should contain valid numeric values.
- Dates should be valid and interpretable.
- Sales should not contain impossible negative values.
- Missing values should be identified before modeling.
- Extreme observations should be investigated rather than automatically deleted.
