# DATA STORY

## Retail Sales: Turning data into decisions

**1. Overall performance:** KPI cards provide a quick view of sales, profit, orders and average order value.

**2. Sales trend:** The monthly line chart shows when sales rise or fall, helping with inventory and campaign planning.

**3. Category performance:** The category ranking identifies high-performing product groups.

**4. Regional performance:** Region comparison highlights stronger and weaker markets.

**5. Profitability:** The sales-vs-profit scatter plot helps check whether higher sales are associated with higher profit.

**6. Customer and payment insights:** Segment and payment charts reveal customer contribution and payment preferences.

## Decision ideas
- Prioritize stock for high-performing categories.
- Investigate weaker regions with targeted marketing.
- Compare sales with profit before increasing promotions.
- Use monthly trends for inventory and campaign planning.
